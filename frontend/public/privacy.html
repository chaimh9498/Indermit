Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026

This policy explains what information Indermit processes and how it is used.

1. Information we collect

We may process account details and identifiers, prompts and uploaded content, generated images, model selections, credit and transaction records, customer-support communications, device and browser information, approximate location derived from an IP address, and security and usage logs.

2. How we use information

We use information to provide and secure the service, authenticate accounts, route requests to selected AI providers, maintain image history, process purchases, restore credits after eligible failures, provide support, prevent fraud and abuse, measure service performance, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Prompts and generated images

Your prompts and generated images are private to your account. Indermit does not use them to train its own AI models or for advertising. They may be transmitted to the model provider you select because that processing is necessary to generate your requested image.

4. Service providers

Indermit uses service providers to operate the platform, including Clerk for authentication, Stripe for payment processing, Cloudflare for infrastructure and security, Google Analytics for website analytics, and the AI model provider selected for a generation. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Payments

Payment information is processed by Stripe. Indermit does not store complete payment-card numbers. We may retain transaction identifiers, payment status, purchased package details, and related accounting records.

6. Retention

Account data and generated images may be retained while your account remains active so your history stays available. You may delete images or request account deletion. Some transaction, security, and compliance records may be retained where reasonably necessary or legally required.

7. Cookies and analytics

Indermit and its providers may use cookies or similar technologies for sign-in, security, preferences, and analytics. Google Analytics helps us understand general site usage and performance.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's privacy

Indermit is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information, contact us so we can review and remove it.

10. Your choices

You may request access to or deletion of your account information and generated content by contacting us. Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights.

11. Changes

We may update this policy as the service develops. The effective date above identifies the latest version.

12. Contact

Privacy questions and requests may be sent to support@indermit.com.
